The pinned evaluation inventory uses the checked-in Reeds/Landini/Stolfi
interlinear release 1.6e6 and Takeshi Takahashi's complete `H` layer. The
[archived release page](https://web.archive.org/web/20030911021322id_/http://www.ic.unicamp.br/~stolfi/voynich/98-12-28-interln16e6/)
identifies the release, basic uncapitalized EVA convention, attribution, and
permission to copy, use, modify, and republish with due credit and no added
restrictions. The exact snapshot, permission URL, normalization, partition
boundary, and outputs are pinned in `evaluation-inventory.v1.json`.
